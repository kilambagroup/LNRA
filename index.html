/ who we are

An operational network
working across markets.

LNRA forms part of the wider operational network behind an international gaming group operating across multiple markets worldwide.

Working closely with teams and partners across different regions, LNRA supports the day-to-day running and ongoing development of the group's brands through a range of operational and business functions.

Operating in diverse regions requires strong coordination, adaptability and collaboration across teams, ensuring the group can continue to scale and respond effectively to different market needs and regulatory environments.
